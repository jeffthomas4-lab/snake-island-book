# The Old Roar — A Sun Lakes Story

A choose-your-own-adventure book about eight cousins at Hotel Sun Lakes, the magical world that lives under the lake, and what it means to belong to a place.

## How to read this

Three ways:

- **[Reading Copy](reading_copy.html)** — The full book in HTML. Every chapter, every region, every path, both endings. Read end-to-end or jump to a chapter. ~50,000 words.
- **[Playable Prototype](prototype.html)** — Click-through CYOA. Make real choices. State tracking, multiple endings. Best for testing how the branches feel.
- **[Bingo Card](bingo_card.html)** — Print this out for the real Sun Lakes trip. 5×5 family-activity card.

## The eight kids

Four sibling pairs, all cousins to each other. Reid & Ella are siblings; Carson & Kiera are siblings and blood cousins to Reid & Ella. Madison & Kellen are siblings (chosen family); Chayten & Aiden are siblings (chosen family). Nobody's family owns the cove. They all rent every July.

- **Madison** — strategist, the oldest, brings the cookies, a dancer with real strength
- **Carson** — competitor, the rally point, big heart under the contest
- **Chayten** — connector, performer, fierce sister, sure on her feet
- **Reid** — craftsman, the calm center, strong in heart and will, knows when to speak
- **Kellen** — athlete and thinker, played whole, lets you see he loves it
- **Kiera** — artist, climber, strong shoulders, sees what others miss
- **Ella** — courage in a small package, the deciding face, fastest of the little ones
- **Aiden** — wonder, kindness, just learned to swim, the youngest

## The story

Hotel Sun Lakes is being threatened by Driedmore Properties — Mr. Drystone wants to drain the cove and build a data center. The dock's foundation is failing. The grown-ups have started believing there's no other option.

The eight cousins find a portal in the sunroom. It opens into The Old Roar — the magical memory-world that lives under the cove. They have until sunset to earn what they need to save Sun Lakes.

Each kid finds their region. Each kid finds their thing. Each kid comes back changed.

Then they walk to the dock and meet Drystone together.
