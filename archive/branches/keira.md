# Chapter 7 - Keira's Branch (9)

~2,000 words / 15-min read for a 3rd grader.

Lead trait: super kind and athletic, tall for her age, competes despite being young, does arts. Both her arts mind AND her competing-with-older-kids show up here, both shine, no weakness exposed.

## What she earns
**The Truth Brush.** A small paint brush that lets her see what's hidden. At the showdown, she's the one who spots Crumb's secret third clipboard - the one with the REAL plans.

## The task (her prize quest)
Paint the Painted Cliff awake by figuring out what he WANTS to be when he wakes up.

## Intro (~300 words)

Keira's raft slides up to the foot of an enormous cliff. Her raft is now a flat wooden raft with a small easel on it, brushes in a holder, and paints in glass jars. The colors are wild - colors she doesn't have names for.

The cliff is gigantic. Black basalt, columns rising hundreds of feet up.

She tilts her head.

The cliff has a face.

She tilts the other way.

The cliff is BREATHING.

She steps off her raft. The cliff doesn't move. But she can hear something. A small sound. Like... snoring? But the snore is COLORED. She can see the color of the snore in the air. Pale blue, with flecks of orange.

She blinks. She still sees the colors.

A small voice from somewhere says, "You're not supposed to be able to see that."

Keira looks around. There's nobody.

The voice says, "I'm UP HERE. Look at the third column from the left."

She looks. The third basalt column has eyes. The eyes are closed.

The cliff is asleep.

He's been asleep, she figures out, for a long time.

He says (still without opening his eyes), "I haven't had a visitor in five hundred years. Are you a visitor or are you a dream? I get them confused."

Keira says, "I'm a visitor."

The cliff says, "WONDERFUL. Could you do me a favor? I would very much like to wake up."

Keira says, "How?"

The cliff says, "I don't know. I've been trying for centuries. Maybe a fresh perspective."

He yawns. The yawn is purple.

## Decision 1: How to start

Keira has paints. She has a wall the size of three football fields. She has a sleeping cliff who wants to wake up.

- **A. Ask the cliff what he wants to be painted as.** (He doesn't know. But the conversation gets them somewhere.)
- **B. Sketch first, then paint.** (Methodical. She studies him while he sleeps.)
- **C. Just start painting and see what happens.** (Bold. Trust the brush.)

All paths lead to her painting him. Each path is a different style.

## Decision 2: The first color

The paints in her jar are alive. They MOVE when she opens them.

- **A. Pick the color that seems to want to come out first.** (The orange leaps out. It paints itself. She just guides it.)
- **B. Pick the color that matches the cliff's snore.** (Blue with orange flecks. Specific. Makes him laugh in his sleep.)
- **C. Pick a color the cliff has never had.** (Pink. She invents pink for him. He's intrigued.)

## Decision 3: Asking him what he wants

While she paints, the cliff talks. He hasn't talked in centuries. He has questions.

- **A. He wants to know what flowers look like up close.** (She paints flowers on his cheekbones.)
- **B. He wants to know what music sounds like.** (She paints sound waves on his arms.)
- **C. He wants to know what KINDNESS looks like.** (She paints something she invents - something that looks like a hand reaching for another hand.)

C is the warmest path. The cliff CRIES paint when she shows him.

## Decision 4: The eyes (Keira's athletic moment)

She gets to his eyes. They're 30 feet up the basalt face. She has to CLIMB.

This is the moment Keira's tall-for-her-age body and her competing-with-older-kids habit shows up in full. She's not afraid of heights. She's been jumping off the small cliff with the older boys for two summers. She studies the rock. She finds the seams in the basalt columns. She climbs.

The mouse with spectacles watches from below, biting his tiny knuckles.

She gets to Cliffy's eye. She has paint and a brush and one hand to work with. The other hand is holding her up.

- **A. Wait for him to open his eyes himself.** (She perches on his cheekbone and waits. He hesitates. She holds his stone hand. He opens.)
- **B. Paint the eyelids first - paint them as if they're already open.** (Trick. He laughs. He opens them for real. She nearly falls. He catches her with one giant slow finger.)
- **C. Sing him a song while she paints around the eyes.** (Music wakes him. He opens his eyes mid-song. She's three feet from his pupil.)

All three paths celebrate her climbing this cliff at age 9. The mouse with spectacles writes the climb into the cliff's history book.

## Decision 5: When he wakes up

He wakes up. He's been asleep so long he doesn't know what to do.

- **A. She introduces herself first.** (Polite. He learns her name. Just the brush, no ally.)
- **B. She asks him his name.** (Together they pick a new one. He chooses "Cliffy." They bond.)
- **C. She lets him just look around.** (He cries paint again. The world is BEAUTIFUL. He hadn't remembered. **Cliffy's PRESENCE comes to the showdown** as a feeling that drifts off the basalt cliffs around the cove. Crumb feels watched. The hairs on his neck stand up. He doesn't know why.)

## Crescendo close (~300 words)

When Cliffy is fully awake, he stands up.

He's the size of a mountain.

He stretches. His joints CRACK in colors. He looks down at Keira and she is the size of his thumbnail.

He says, "You did this. You PAINTED me awake. You're the first person in five hundred years to even SEE me."

Keira shrugs. "I just looked carefully."

Cliffy reaches into his own basalt chest. He pulls out a small brush. The brush is wood, with a tip made of light.

"This," he says, "is a Truth Brush. You won't paint with it. You'll just LOOK through it. Anything hidden becomes visible. Lies turn yellow. Truth glows blue."

Keira takes the brush. It fits in her pocket.

Cliffy kneels (slowly - it takes a minute). His face is enormous and kind.

"If you ever need a giant," he says, "stand somewhere with stone and call my name."

Keira says, "What's your name again?"

"Cliffy."

"That's a great name."

"I know."

He winks at her, which means a whole canyon's worth of basalt blinks at once. It is amazing.

She climbs back onto her raft. The easel is folded. The paints are corked. The Truth Brush is in her pocket.

As she paddles off, Cliffy yawns again. This time he doesn't sleep. He just looks around at the world he hadn't seen in five hundred years. He smiles a smile the size of a mountainside.

## How this feeds the showdown
Keira's SEEING exposes what Crumb is hiding. The Truth Brush amplifies, but she's the one whose eye catches the wrong thing first. Her arts mind reads people the way she reads a cliff face - she looks for what's underneath the surface. Her trait wins this beat. The brush is just the magnifier.

She holds it up to her eye. She looks at Crumb.

Crumb is YELLOW. His clipboards are YELLOW. His permits are YELLOW.

Madison's map glows BLUE.

Keira says, simply, "He's lying."

The crowd doesn't see what she sees. They see her certainty. The wave of belief turns the crowd against Crumb.

If the reader picked Decision 5C, **Cliffy's presence drifts off the basalt cliffs** that ring the cove. Crumb keeps glancing up nervously. The hairs on his neck stand up. He doesn't know why.

## Notes
- Keira is 9 but she's tall and she competes with older kids. Her branch celebrates her exactly as she is - young, but able to take down something a thousand times her size.
- Cliffy is the gentlest giant in the book. He never threatens. He is sleepy, kind, and in awe of the world.
- The Truth Brush is also a great metaphor for noticing - seeing what's actually there.
