# Chapter 1 - Framing - The Sunroom

Opening chapter. ~2,000 words / 15-min read at 3rd grade level. Establishes Hotel Sun Lakes, all eight kids, the trip, the real-world threat from Driedmore Properties, and ends with the eight kids crossing into The Old Roar.

## Beats

### Open at the dock (intro, ~250 words)
All eight kids on screen fast, each doing their signature thing. None of them is on a screen - they haven't been on a screen in three days. That's the rule at Sun Lakes. Bedrock rule. The kids don't even mind anymore.

- Madison reads on the grass with a tin of cookies next to her
- Chayten is paddle-boarding back from the back dock with Aiden, who's holding a fishing rod
- Reid is whittling a stick at the edge of the dock
- Kellen is throwing a ball into the water and swimming after it
- Carson is hyping up a mini-golf rematch
- Keira is sketching the lake in a notebook
- Ella is doing her makeup with a hand mirror, queenly
- Aiden is talking to his fishing rod

The brisket smell drifts down from the main house. The lake is hot and bright.

Reid steps off the third plank of the main dock. The plank wobbles. He looks down. The whole foundation is shifting where the water has eaten away at the dirt around the pilings. He can see it. He's seen it for a couple of summers now. It's getting worse.

He doesn't say anything. There's nothing to say.

### Mr. Crumb arrives (~300 words)
A man in a hard hat shows up at the dock with three clipboards. He says he's from Driedmore Properties. He's putting up a sign.

The sign says:

> COMING SOON
> DRIEDMORE DATA CENTER - WEST CASCADIA HUB
> GROUNDBREAKING JULY 4 - 12:00 NOON
> THIS COVE IS UNDER CONTRACT TO DRIEDMORE PROPERTIES

Madison reads it twice. She knows what a data center is. She knows what they need.

Carson asks. Madison says, "They use a LOT of water. Cooling. They want to drain the lake."

Aiden says, "But we can't have a lake if there's no water."

Madison says, "That's the whole point."

Crumb sees the kids and says something dismissive ("don't touch the sign, kids, this here is a malfeasance of zoning"). He pronounces "ecosystem" "echo-system." He's looking at his tablet the whole time. He never looks at the kids' faces.

Reid asks why his family is selling.

Crumb laughs. "Selling? Sweetheart, your dock's FOUNDATION is gone. The water's eaten the ground out from under it. Your family can't AFFORD the geological work. Driedmore is rescuing them. We're the GOOD GUYS here."

He scans something on his tablet. He doesn't look up.

He leaves.

The kids look at the dock. The pilings underneath are leaning. The water near the bank is swirling in a way that pulls dirt into the lake.

The dock foundation IS being eroded.

The kids look at each other.

Madison says, quietly, "We have to do something."

### The kids try to do something and can't (~250 words)
They try the obvious things.

- Madison wants to find the adults but the adults are inside making brisket and won't come out for another hour, AND she knows the adults already heard about the deal and are quietly considering it because of the dock.
- Reid suggests pulling the sign down. Carson says no, that's not how you win.
- Kellen suggests calling someone. Keira points out they don't know who.
- Aiden asks if they can just ASK the lake to stop it.

A pause. They laugh at Aiden's idea. But Madison doesn't laugh. She looks at the sunroom.

### The sunroom acts weird (~300 words)
The kids drift toward the main house. The sunroom door is open and the rafts are inside, piled up. The big inflated unicorn is on top.

The unicorn is bobbing.

There's no wind.

Aiden walks in first, because Aiden is six and has not yet learned to be afraid of weird things. The other seven follow.

The room smells like brisket and warm plastic and something else - something WET, like cold lake water on a hot day, even though there's no water in the room.

### The C-130 (~300 words)
Then the C-130 comes.

It's the same time it comes every year. Low through the canyon. So loud the windows rattle. Aiden grabs Chayten's hand. Reid grins. Carson covers his ears and laughs.

The plane thunders past. The sunroom shakes. The rafts move.

When the rumble fades, the rafts are NOT where they were.

The unicorn raft is on the floor. Madison's canoe-shaped raft is in front of her. Each kid is standing in front of a raft, like the rafts were waiting for them.

Aiden climbs onto the unicorn first. He doesn't think about it. He just does.

The unicorn lifts off the floor.

### Eight kids, eight rafts, eight worlds (~400 words)
One by one, each kid climbs onto their raft. The room stretches. The walls of the sunroom go away. The lake outside isn't the lake outside anymore.

There's a bigger water. A LOUDER water.

Madison hears something - a big sound far away. Like a waterfall.

Like the BIGGEST waterfall.

Each raft slides off in a different direction, into a different part of an enormous, watery, green world. The kids try to call to each other but the wind is loud.

Madison's voice is the last thing each one hears: "WHATEVER YOU DO, STAY ON YOUR RAFT UNTIL IT LANDS."

(Madison is the oldest. She always knows.)

### Crescendo close (~200 words)
End on Aiden, smallest, on the unicorn raft, riding through air that smells like green and water. He looks back. His brother and sister and cousins are gone, each headed somewhere different. He should be scared.

He's not.

He's smiling so hard his cheeks hurt.

The unicorn raft turns its big inflatable head and says,

"LOOK AT THIS!"

End chapter.

> Madison (oldest, the strategist) - turn to chapter 2
> Chayten (the actress) - turn to chapter 3

> Reid (the gentle giant) - turn to chapter 5
> Kellen (the athlete) - turn to chapter 6

> Aiden (the youngest) - turn to chapter 9
>
