# Chapter 2 - Madison's Branch (15)

~2,000 words / 15-min read for a 3rd grader. Intro + decision points + crescendo close.

Lead trait: oldest, parental authority, strategic eye while always helping. Makes the best cookies. Former ballet.

## What she earns
**The Map.** A real-deal map of Mr. Crumb's actual Driedmore Properties plans. Strategic intel that opens the showdown.

## The task (her prize quest)
Find three hidden map fragments scattered across The Old Roar and put them together.

## Intro (~300 words)
Madison's raft slides to a stop on the shore of a perfectly still pool. Her raft has become a wooden canoe with her name carved into the side: *MADISON*. The tin of cookies she was holding is still in her lap. None of the cookies are crushed.

The pool is so quiet she can hear her own heartbeat.

Three heads pop up out of the water. Three girls. One looks her age. One looks twelve. One looks ten. They all have hair that drips upward instead of down.

The oldest one says: "Are you the cookie-bringer?"

The middle one says: "She IS the cookie-bringer."

The youngest one is already chewing on something.

They climb out of the pool. Their feet leave wet prints that turn into flowers.

The oldest says: "I'm Pip. That's Lark. The one chewing is Wren. Wren, what are you eating."

Wren shows her hands. Her hands are full of pondweed she's shaped into a sad-looking cookie.

"It's my OWN recipe," Wren says.

"It's TERRIBLE," Pip says.

Madison opens her tin. She holds out a real cookie.

All three Lake Sisters reach for it at once.

### Decision 1: How does she share the cookies?

- **A. Give them one each, fairly.** (They're impressed. "Fair is good, fair is RIGHT," Pip says.)
- **B. Give the youngest the biggest one.** (Wren cries with joy. Pip and Lark laugh. They love Madison.)
- **C. Tell them they have to earn it by helping her.** (They love this. They're game-players. "WE LIKE YOU," Lark says.)

All three paths land at the same place: Madison and the Lake Sisters become friends.

## Decision 2: The Sisters tell her the threat (~300 words)

The Sisters say they've been watching the lake-up-above for years. They know about Mr. Crumb. They've seen his boats. They've seen him drop pieces of paper into the water. The pieces of paper sank. The Lake Sisters caught them and hid them in three different places in The Old Roar.

The papers are pieces of his REAL plans. Not the public sign. The real plans.

Pip says: "The papers want to be put back together. Will you help?"

Madison says yes.

- **A. She wants to know the threat first.** (Pip shows her a vision in the pool: Crumb's bulldozers, the dam, the dry lakebed.)
- **B. She wants to know the plan first.** (Lark gives her a route map. Three places. Three sisters. Three guides.)
- **C. She wants to know what's at stake first.** (Wren shows her the magical creatures of the lake who would lose their home.)

All paths converge: the Sisters split up to take her to the three locations.

## Decision 3: Which sister to go with first

Each leads to a different mini-adventure. Madison can pick the order. The choices are real because each location has a different texture.

- **A. Pip and the Singing Cave.** A cave that hums when you sing into it. The map fragment is wedged in a crack only reachable by harmonizing with the cave. Madison's voice from her old ballet recitals comes back.
- **B. Lark and the Otter Bridge.** A bridge made of sleeping otters. The map fragment is under the third otter. Madison has to wake the right otter without disturbing the others. Strategy game.
- **C. Wren and the Cookie Trade.** A grumpy beaver named Branch has the third fragment and won't give it up for nothing. Wren says "trade him a cookie!" Madison has to decide what to trade and what NOT to trade.

She does all three in the order she picks. The book gives the option to read them all or skip ahead after one or two. (For a kid who wants the full Madison adventure, all three.)

## Decision 4: At the third location, Branch the beaver wants more than just a cookie

Branch is lonely. He wants company more than he wants pastry.

- **A. Trade a cookie.** (Branch eats it, gives map fragment, waves goodbye. No ally at showdown.)
- **B. Stay and have a tea party with Branch.** (Costs her time. **Branch becomes her friend. He SHOWS UP at the showdown** to corroborate her testimony as a witness who lives in the lake.)
- **C. Invite Branch to come along.** (Branch declines but gives her a beaver-tooth charm. Charm appears in coda. No showdown ally but a sequel hook.)

All paths get the fragment.

## Decision 5: Putting the map together

Back at the Quiet Pool. The Sisters help her assemble the three fragments.

The map shows:

- The drainage path - the data center will need ALL of the cove's water, plus more
- The fake "rescue" appraisal - Driedmore lowballed the value because the bank they're using is also Driedmore's
- The wrong permit type they filed - the loophole

That last bit is the real prize.

- **A. Madison memorizes it.** (Reliable. She can recite it from memory at the showdown.)
- **B. Madison rolls it up and brings the paper.** (Physical evidence. Tactile prop.)
- **C. Madison asks the Sisters to put a copy in her cookie tin.** (The tin is now magical. The cookies smell like cookies AND like the lake. The tin glows faintly when she opens it at the showdown. Strongest version.)

All three work at the showdown. The strongest version makes the moment more visual.

## Crescendo close (~300 words)

The Sisters walk her to the edge of the Quiet Pool. The water is rising in a way that feels like a goodbye.

Pip says: "Tell our older sister hi."

Madison says: "Who's your older sister?"

Lark says: "The big one. The one your hotel is named for. The cove."

Madison realizes the lake at home - their lake - is family to these girls. Has always been. The chosen family thing the kids talk about all the time goes BACK. Way back.

Wren hands her one more cookie - a pondweed-and-real-cookie hybrid that Wren baked herself. It's terrible.

Madison eats it anyway. She smiles. Wren bursts into happy tears.

Madison's canoe is waiting.

She steps in.

She has the map. She has the loophole. She has three sisters in The Old Roar who are now part of her chosen family forever.

The canoe pushes off. The Sisters wave from the water. Their hair drips upward.

Madison heads back toward the sound of the C-130, faint and far, somewhere up in the world above.

## How this feeds the showdown
Madison is the FIRST kid to step forward at the data center groundbreaking. Her STRATEGY orders the showdown - she's the one who knew exactly what would unravel Crumb's pitch and in what order. The map is the document her strategy points to. She doesn't shout. She just talks plainly and the adults can't help but listen. That's her trait at full volume.

If the reader picked Decision 4B, **Branch the beaver waddles up the dock and corroborates her testimony as a witness who lives in the lake**.

## Notes
- Madison's ballet shows up in how she moves through the cave (Decision 3A) and the otter bridge (3B). Not narrated, just shown.
- Her cookies are currency, weapon, gift, and bridge between worlds.
- "Chosen family" gets stated in-world by the Lake Sisters - the lake itself recognizes the trip's meaning.
- Branch the beaver could be a recurring side character if Jeff wants a sequel.
