# Chapter 10 - The Showdown - Eight Against Crumb

~4,500 words / 30-min read for a 3rd grader. The longest chapter. The payoff. After the eight branches, all eight kids return through the sunroom together. They have their eight earned things. Crumb's data center groundbreaking is in fifteen minutes. They walk down to the dock together.

## Structure overview
- Return through the sunroom
- The walk to the dock
- Beats 1-4 (Madison, Chayten, Carson, Reid)
- **CRUMB PUSHBACK** (Crumb pulls his ace - the bank offer)
- Beats 5-8 (Kellen, Keira, Ella, Aiden)
- The C-130 flyover at climax
- Coda: sparklers, snap-its, the dock looks better in the morning

## Beats

### Return through the sunroom (~300 words)
Each kid lands back in the sunroom from a different direction. The rafts are normal again - just plastic. Big inflated unicorn is propped against the wall.

Except the kids are NOT normal.

- Madison has a tin of cookies that smells like the lake AND like cookies. The map is rolled inside.
- Chayten has a small mask in her pocket, otter-sized, made of driftwood. Some readers also have an otter top hat.
- Carson has a tusk-charm on a leather cord around his neck.
- Reid has a wooden staff he didn't have an hour ago.
- Kellen has a flat stone in his palm.
- Keira has a small brush behind her ear.
- Ella has a crown invisible to most people.
- Aiden has a unicorn standing behind him.

Pegasus says, "Is THIS the lake-up-above?"

The kids look at each other. They start laughing.

(For readers who picked the warmest paths, additional friends are also somewhere nearby - Branch the beaver in the hallway, Otto the otter in a window, Big Coulee outside the back wall, Old Speak as a thicket of new young trees that wasn't there yesterday, Skim and her flock in the yard, the egg-mother and her chorus near the dock, Marigold's frog herald sitting primly on the porch, the Hush clinging to Aiden's shoe.)

### The walk to the dock (~300 words)
Eight kids walk down to the dock. A unicorn walks behind them, invisible to anyone over 18. The brisket smell follows them. A C-130 had thundered overhead earlier in the week.

The construction kickoff is set up at the end of the dock. Small platform. Folding chairs (mostly empty). Bulldozer parked on the dirt road. Banner: DRIEDMORE DATA CENTER - WEST CASCADIA HUB - GROUNDBREAKING JULY 4.

A modest crowd. Some adults from town. Some hotel guests. The kids' parents off to one side, talking quietly. They look worried. They look like adults who have been told there's no other option.

The kids' parents have not seen the kids yet.

Mr. Crumb is at the platform with three clipboards (the fourth is hidden inside his jacket). He's mid-speech.

He's saying things like "premium server infrastructure" and "echo-system improvement" and "fiscal opportunity for the cove." He keeps glancing at his tablet.

The eight kids walk up to the platform. Pegasus hangs back at the corner of the dock, invisible. The other allies (depending on the reader's path) are positioned around the perimeter.

Crumb sees the kids. He sighs.

"Kids," he says, "this is a GROWN-UP event. Run along."

The crowd laughs nervously.

Madison steps forward.

### Beat 1: Madison (~350 words)

Madison's STRATEGY orders the showdown. She's the one who knew exactly what would unravel Crumb's pitch and in what order.

She pulls out the rolled-up map from her cookie tin. She unrolls it on the platform. Her cookie tin glows faintly. She doesn't shout. She just talks plainly.

She says, "Mr. Crumb. This is a copy of your real plans. Not the public sign. The real ones. They show the water draw rate, the drainage path, and the wrong permit you filed."

Crumb stutters. He says, "Where did you GET that."

Madison says, "From a friend who lives in the lake."

Crumb laughs. "From a friend who LIVES in the lake. Right."

The crowd is half-laughing too. The map looks official but they're not sure.

(If reader picked Branch's path) **Branch the beaver waddles up onto the platform**. He's wearing a small bow tie. He says, in a creaky beaver voice, "I live in the lake." The crowd goes very quiet.

Madison continues. She names the water draw - millions of gallons a day, every day, forever. She names the bank - Driedmore owns it, the appraisal was rigged. She names the wrong permit type. The map shows everything.

Some of the parents start listening. They had been told this was the only way. They had not been told it would mean draining the lake.

### Beat 2: Chayten (~350 words)

Chayten's WORDS hold the crowd. The mask amplifies, but she's the speaker. Her voice carries because it's truthful.

She steps up to the makeshift podium. She slips the otter mask out of her pocket. The crowd doesn't see it - they just feel something change.

She says, "Listen to her."

Just three words. The crowd STOPS shifting. Stops fidgeting. Everyone's eyes lock on Chayten.

She says, "Listen to all of us. We have something to say. It is the truth and you should hear it."

Behind her, in the back of the crowd, Aiden is holding a small fish that Chayten brought back for him from her chapter. He smiles at her. She smiles back. The whole reason her voice carries is that she's standing here for HIM and for the rest of them. The mask just lets her speak loud enough for the crowd to hear.

Madison continues. She names the data center plan. She names what it would cost the lake. She names what the family was going to lose.

The crowd is listening.

Crumb tries to interrupt.

The crowd looks at him with annoyance.

He shuts up.

(If reader picked Otto's path) **Otto and the Driftwood Players appear** at the back of the dock and start a slow rhythmic clapping. Whenever Crumb tries to speak, the otters clap louder. He cannot get a word in edgewise.

### Beat 3: Carson (~300 words)

Crumb gathers himself. He yells, "This is RIDICULOUS. I'm starting the bulldozer."

He stomps over to it.

Carson steps in front of the bulldozer. His PRESENCE is what rallies the kids. He doesn't have to say anything. He just stands there.

Reid lines up beside him. Then Kellen. Then Keira and Ella. Then Madison and Chayten and Aiden (Pegasus hangs back, still invisible).

Eight kids in a line in front of a bulldozer.

The tusk-charm at Carson's neck glows. The OTHER kids feel it. They feel like a TEAM. But the team was forming because Carson was there, not because of the charm. The charm just makes the bond visible.

Crumb says, "I am calling the police."

Carson says, "Go ahead. Call them. Show them your real plans."

(If reader picked path B or C with Big Coulee) **Big Coulee thunders down the dock road**, flower crown still tilted over one eye, narrating her own approach: "AND BIG COULEE arrives, looking STRONG, looking READY, looking like the AUNTIE these kids NEED."

(If 5C, also Tuft and Tumble.) Three mammoths on the dock. Crumb stares. The crowd stares. The kids' parents stare. (Also the parents can SEE Big Coulee. The unicorn-invisibility-to-adults rule is just for Pegasus. Big Coulee is a wooly mammoth and there's no hiding her.)

### Beat 4: Reid (~300 words)

Crumb climbs into the bulldozer. He starts it up. The engine roars.

The kids don't move.

Reid steps forward and plants the unbreakable staff in the dirt right in front of the bulldozer's tracks.

Reid's CALM is what holds the line. The staff is just where his calm lives.

The bulldozer rolls forward.

It hits the staff.

The staff doesn't move.

The bulldozer's tracks SPIN. Smoke comes off them. The bulldozer doesn't go anywhere.

Crumb keeps revving. The staff stays planted.

Reid says, calmly, "It's not going to move."

The crowd actually laughs. The bulldozer keeps spinning. Crumb is sweating through his hard hat.

(If reader picked Old Speak's path) **A thicket of trees that wasn't there an hour ago is now standing at the edge of the dock**. The trees are tall and quiet. They lean slightly toward Crumb. The kids' parents look at the trees and try to remember if those trees were always there.

### CRUMB PUSHBACK (~500 words)

Crumb climbs out of the bulldozer. He's furious. He grabs his three clipboards. He looks at the crowd. He looks at the kids. He looks at the kids' parents.

He plays his ace.

He pulls out a fourth piece of paper - a SIGNED OFFER LETTER from the family's bank.

"This family," Crumb says, "is BROKE. Their dock's foundation is GONE. The water is eating it out from under them. They cannot AFFORD the geological work. The bank is calling their loan. Driedmore is the ONLY buyer. We are RESCUING them."

Some of the adults in the crowd nod. Some of the parents close their eyes.

The kids freeze.

This is the real fight. Not the bulldozer. Not the sign. The MONEY. The grown-ups had been told there was no other way and the grown-ups had believed it because the dock IS falling apart. They could SEE it falling apart.

Crumb sees the kids' faces. He smiles a thin, mean smile.

"I'm sorry, kids," he says, even though he isn't. "Sometimes the world doesn't work out the way you want. Sometimes the GROWN-UPS have to make the hard call."

Aiden's bottom lip wobbles.

Pegasus, invisible behind the platform, leans her unicorn head against Aiden's shoulder. Aiden steadies.

Madison steps forward again.

She opens the cookie tin. The map is inside. So is something else.

She points at the offer letter Crumb is holding.

She says, "Look at the bank's name."

Crumb says, "It's a BANK. Banks have NAMES."

Madison says, "Look at the holding company. Top of the page. Small print."

Crumb looks. The crowd squints.

The bank is owned by Driedmore Holdings.

Madison says, "They lowballed the appraisal because they're buying from themselves. The 'rescue' is a robbery."

The kids' parents look at each other. The adults in the crowd start murmuring.

Crumb says, "That's a LIE. That's a CHILD'S misunderstanding."

The kids freeze again.

This is the moment Kellen steps in.

### Beat 5: Kellen (~350 words)

Kellen's THINKING is what wins this beat. He's the one who saw where Crumb hid the third clipboard - and now the fourth one inside Crumb's jacket. He watched Crumb's posture all morning. He watched where Crumb's hand kept drifting.

The stone is just the messenger Kellen sends. Anyone could throw a rock. Only Kellen knew exactly where to throw THIS one.

He pulls out the stone.

He throws.

The stone skips off the wood of the dock. Bounces off a railing. Skips up the steps of Crumb's platform. Lands on Crumb's THIRD clipboard - the secret one - and balances on its edge.

The clipboard tips open. Inside: Driedmore Holdings ownership of the bank. Internal memos about lowballing appraisals. The actual data center water draw rates.

The crowd reads it. The crowd gasps.

The kids' parents are now VERY interested.

(If reader picked Skim's path) **Skim and her flock fly in** like a small precision air force and skip stones onto every clipboard Crumb has hidden, including the FOURTH one in his jacket. Total exposure. Crumb's hat falls off. Both clipboards are now public.

### Beat 6: Keira (~300 words)

Crumb tries to claim someone planted the third clipboard. He says he's never seen it before. He says the kids are LIARS.

Keira's SEEING exposes the lie. Her arts mind reads people the way she reads a cliff face - looking for what's underneath. Her trait wins this beat. The brush is just the magnifier.

She pulls the Truth Brush from behind her ear. She holds it up to her eye. She looks at Crumb.

He's bright YELLOW. The clipboards are YELLOW. The offer letter is YELLOW.

Madison's map glows BLUE.

Keira says, simply, "He's lying."

She says it small but everyone hears it.

A grown-up in the crowd says, "Wait, can you see something we can't?"

Keira says, "Yes. He's lying. I can see it."

The crowd believes her. They believe her because she's clearly telling the truth herself.

(If reader picked Cliffy's path) **Cliffy's presence drifts off the basalt cliffs** that ring the cove. Crumb keeps glancing up nervously. The hairs on his neck stand up. He doesn't know why.

### Beat 7: Ella (~300 words)

Crumb is purple in the face. He shouts, "I HAVE A PERMIT. THE PERMIT IS LEGAL. I AM PROCEEDING."

Ella steps in front of him. She's eight years old. She's smaller than him by half.

Her COURAGE is what stops him. The crown is symbolic. What actually moves him is an eight-year-old refusing to flinch.

The invisible crown becomes visible to Crumb for one second. He sees it. He doesn't know what he's seeing.

She says, "I am Queen Ella of the Bright Water. And the answer is NO."

Crumb's mouth opens. His mouth closes.

He cannot find a single word.

The crowd applauds.

(If reader picked path B or C with the egg-mother) **The egg-mother and her chorus appear** behind Ella, a small audience of water creatures who hum a single note of condemnation. Crumb hears it. The other adults don't, but they see Ella's face and they see Crumb's face and they don't need to hear the water to know what's happening.

(If 4C with Marigold) **A frog in tiny royal regalia hops onto the platform**. He bows. He produces a small scroll. He reads, in a voice that's clearer than most grown-ups: "By order of Her Majesty Marigold, Queen of the Bright Water, Hotel Sun Lakes is hereby declared Protected Territory of the Realm. Any party who attempts to alter this cove will answer to the Bright Water Court." He bows again. He hops back into the lake.

The kids' parents are speechless.

### Beat 8: Aiden (~400 words)

Crumb makes one last try. He says, "I want PROOF this is real magic. PROVE TO ME magic exists. Otherwise this is all a child's TANTRUM."

Aiden walks forward. He's been at the back the whole time, holding Pegasus's mane.

His BELIEF is what makes Pegasus visible to Crumb specifically. Adults can't normally see her. But when Aiden looks Crumb in the eye and BELIEVES, fully and without hedging, that there is a real unicorn standing on the dock - his belief is strong enough to make her real for Crumb. Just for Crumb. Just for that moment.

That's a six-year-old's super power. He believes harder than any adult ever could.

He whistles.

Pegasus walks up the dock.

Aiden looks Crumb dead in the eye.

Crumb sees her. Just Crumb. The other adults don't see her. But Crumb does.

A real, talking, rainbow-maned unicorn standing three feet away.

His clipboards drop. His hard hat falls off again. His face goes white.

Aiden says, "Mr. Crumb. This is Pegasus. She's a unicorn."

Pegasus says, "HI!"

She trots up to Crumb and sniffs him.

Crumb opens his mouth. He almost says "There's a unicorn." He stops. He realizes if he says that, the other adults will think HE'S lost his mind.

He has to choose between admitting the magic and his career.

He chooses his career. He whispers, "I... can't... I can't put this on a permit."

Aiden says, "We know."

Crumb crumples. The crowd can't see what broke him, but they can see CRUMB - white-faced, sweating, beaten. He drops the offer letter.

The deal is dead.

Pegasus says, "LOOK AT ALL OF THEM!"

(If reader picked Hush's path) **The Hush is also at the showdown**, riding on Aiden's shoe, visible only to the eight kids. The kids share looks of "is this real?" all afternoon.

### The C-130 (~250 words)

The construction is over. Crumb's plan is dead. The Driedmore lawyers will have to sort out the bank fraud thing.

But it's not the end yet.

The C-130 thunders overhead. Mid-week flyover, right on schedule.

Every adult and every kid on the dock looks up. The plane comes through the canyon huge and low.

Pegasus rears up onto her hind legs. She watches the plane. The plane is louder than she has ever heard anything be.

When it passes, Pegasus says, "ANOTHER ONE!"

Aiden says, "There's another one tomorrow."

Pegasus is delighted.

The kids' parents look at the kids. Then at the dock. Then back at the kids. They don't know what just happened. They know SOMETHING just happened.

Madison walks over to her parents. She says, "We figured it out. The dock is going to be okay."

Her parents look at each other.

They look at the dock.

They want to ask. They don't know how.

### Coda: The dock heals overnight (~300 words)

That night.

Sparklers and snap-its on the dock.

Madison is sharing cookies with Aiden. He gives one to Pegasus, who is hidden in the sunroom (the unicorn is staying for the rest of the trip - she'll go back to The Old Roar after the kids leave).

Reid is whittling.

Kellen is throwing something into the lake.

Keira is sketching the sunset.

Ella is wearing her invisible crown.

Carson is hyping a mini-golf rematch for tomorrow.

Chayten is on the back dock with Aiden's fishing rod, casting in the dim light.

The brisket is gone. The fire is low. The lake is dark and the stars are coming up.

Reid walks over to the bank where the dock pilings meet the water. He plants his Driftwood staff in the ground at the base of the third piling. The staff sinks half its length into the soft earth. The staff hums quietly. The water around it changes its flow - just a little, just enough.

He doesn't say anything. He walks back.

In the morning, the dock looks slightly better. Boards seem tighter. The third plank's gap is smaller. The water near the bank is no longer pulling dirt away. The pilings have settled.

The kids notice. The adults don't.

The eight kids look at each other across the dock.

They know what they did today.

The grown-ups don't.

Madison smiles. She holds up a cookie.

Aiden holds up his snap-it.

Pegasus, in the sunroom, says quietly, "LOOK AT THIS."

The data center is dead. The cove is safe. The dock will hold for the rest of the summer. And for the rest of the summers after that.

End of book.

## Notes
- Each beat in the showdown is short. The kid reader gets EIGHT distinct moments of "their kid wins."
- Crumb pushes back ONCE (the bank offer) and the kids dig deeper to defeat it. That's the only friction. He's never scary, just briefly above the kids' heads, and Madison's map handles it.
- Crumb is humiliated but never harmed. He just leaves with no clipboards and no plan.
- Pegasus stays for the rest of the trip - sets up a sequel and gives Aiden's family a magical secret. Adults can't see her. Only the eight kids can.
- The chosen family theme lands at the end: eight kids, one chosen team, sharing one cookie under a Fourth of July sky.
- The dock-heals coda is the kid-secret ending. They didn't just save the trip from being sold. They healed the actual problem.
- Magical residue stabilizing the foundation explains in-world why the dock holds going forward. Adults figure it must have been a lucky water shift. Kids know better.
