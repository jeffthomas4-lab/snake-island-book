## Placement decisions for Atticus

- **Chapter openers** — each chapter (Arrival, eight cousin chapters, Gather, Showdown, Endings) gets one full-page or half-page illustration as the opener. We have 11 chapters and 24 images, so most chapters get 1-2 images, some get the establishing shot at the start and a payoff image later.
- **Interior images** — use the magic-world and showdown images at the moment they pay off in prose, not as decorative inserts.
- **Map page** — front matter, after the dedication. Use map.png at half-page width.
- **Cover** — front cover; spine and back cover need separate art if going to KDP.
