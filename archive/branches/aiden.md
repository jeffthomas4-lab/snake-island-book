# Chapter 9 - Aiden's Branch (6)

~2,000 words / 15-min read for a 3rd grader. Most magical, most wonder-filled. Designed to be read aloud easily for a six-year-old.

Lead trait: pure heart, kindness, knows right from wrong, the youngest. Wants to fish on the back dock. The moral compass of the eight.

## What he earns
**The Unicorn.** Pegasus (working name) the unicorn comes back with him. A real, live, talking unicorn in the real world. Mr. Crumb cannot put a unicorn on a permit.

## The task (his prize quest)
Help the unicorn save a baby river otter who fell down a magic hole. Be the rider she's been waiting for.

## Intro (~300 words)

Aiden's raft is the unicorn raft. He climbed onto it in the sunroom and now he's flying.

The big inflatable unicorn used to be plastic. Now she's REAL. Her mane is rainbow. Her hooves don't quite touch the ground.

She turns her big head and looks at Aiden over her shoulder.

"LOOK AT THIS!" she says. (She points with her horn at a cloud shaped like a fish.)

Aiden says, "Yeah!"

She says, "AND LOOK AT THIS!" (Now she's pointing at a rainbow that's growing sideways.)

Aiden says, "Wow!"

She lands gently in a meadow. Aiden slides off her back. She bows her head so they're eye to eye.

She says, "I have been waiting for a rider for two hundred years. Did you know that? Do you want to know why? Because no one else FIT. The riders were too big. Or too tall. Or too OLD. Or they didn't BELIEVE in unicorns enough. And then today I felt a small boy climbing onto me in a sunroom and I thought, OH. THERE he is."

She tells him her name. She has never said it out loud before.

Pegasus.

Aiden says, "But Pegasus is a HORSE not a UNICORN."

Pegasus says, "I picked it MYSELF."

Aiden laughs. Pegasus is delighted.

Then she gets serious. "Aiden," she says. "There's a problem in The Old Roar. A baby otter fell into a magic hole. Only a unicorn can save her. But I am too YOUNG to save her by myself. I need a rider. Will you ride with me?"

Aiden doesn't even hesitate.

"Yes."

## Decision 1: How to find the hole

Pegasus knows it's in one of three places.

- **A. The Cliffside.** (The hole is at the base of a basalt cliff. Hard to reach.)
- **B. The Meadow of Bells.** (The hole is hidden under a flower. Easy to find but tricky to navigate.)
- **C. The River's Bend.** (The hole is in the river itself. Wet adventure.)

The book lets all three be valid. Each one has a different scenery.

## Decision 2: The hole

When they find it, the hole is bigger than expected. Inside, they can hear the baby otter crying.

The otter's name is Ripple. She's three weeks old.

- **A. Pegasus jumps in first.** (Brave but they're separated.)
- **B. Aiden gets lowered in by Pegasus's mane.** (Teamwork. Pegasus stretches her mane like a rope.)
- **C. They go in together.** (Pegasus shrinks - just a little - and they descend together.)

## Decision 3: The bottom of the hole

It's not a hole. It's a CAVE that opens into a glittering room. Ripple is in the middle of the room. She's safe but she can't get out. The room has FOUR doors and Ripple doesn't know which is the way back up.

- **A. Pegasus uses her horn to glow.** (One door glows back. That's the way.)
- **B. Aiden asks Ripple which door SOUNDS like home.** (Ripple listens. She picks one.)
- **C. Aiden notices the floor has tiny prints leading to one door.** (Ripple's own prints from when she fell.)

## Decision 4: A challenge in the way

The right door is guarded by a small grumpy creature called a Hush. It doesn't want anyone to leave through its door because it's lonely.

The Hush is smaller than Aiden. It looks sad more than scary.

- **A. Aiden asks the Hush to come with them.** (Hush is OVERWHELMED with joy. It's never been invited anywhere. **Hush SHOWS UP at the showdown** as a tiny side character who clings to Aiden's leg.)
- **B. Aiden gives the Hush a hug.** (Hush has never been hugged. It steps aside, weeping with happiness.)
- **C. Aiden tells the Hush a fishing story.** (Aiden talks about the back dock at home. Hush is enchanted. Sequel hook - Hush and Aiden write letters back and forth.)

All three paths get through. All three give Aiden a tiny new friend.

## Decision 5: Getting Ripple home

They climb out. Pegasus carries Ripple in her mane. They reach the surface.

Ripple's family is waiting. Her mother is so relieved she can't speak. She just touches Aiden's nose with her own.

- **A. Aiden goes home now.** (Pegasus and Aiden say goodbye.)
- **B. Aiden asks Pegasus if she can come with him.** (She thinks. She nods. She CAN. But it's a big choice.)
- **C. Aiden stays for a tea party with the otter family.** (They have a small celebration. Then he asks Pegasus to come.)

The strongest version is when Aiden invites Pegasus to come back with him. Without that, the showdown is missing its biggest move.

## Crescendo close (~300 words)

They are flying back to the lake-up-above.

Pegasus says, "Aiden. Are you sure you want me to come?"

Aiden says, "Yes."

Pegasus says, "It might be SCARY. The man with the clipboards is going to be there."

Aiden says, "Yeah, but Madison is going to be there. And Chayten. And Carson and Reid and Kellen and Keira and Ella. We'll all be there."

Pegasus is quiet for a moment.

She says, "I have never met seven other children before."

Aiden says, "You'll like them."

Pegasus says, "I'll be the only unicorn."

Aiden says, "We'll all be unicorns. We just won't have horns. We'll just be... unicorns INSIDE."

Pegasus laughs the loudest unicorn laugh in two hundred years.

They fly higher. The C-130 is rumbling somewhere in the world above. Pegasus glides toward the sound.

She lands gently in the sunroom. Aiden slides off her back.

The other seven kids are there. They all look at the unicorn.

The unicorn looks at them.

Madison says, "Is that...?"

Aiden says, "Her name is Pegasus. She's coming with us to the dock."

Pegasus says, "LOOK AT ALL OF THEM!"

The kids all start laughing at once.

Outside, the C-130 thunders past. The construction kickoff is in fifteen minutes.

## How this feeds the showdown
Aiden's BELIEF is what makes Pegasus visible to Crumb specifically. Adults can't see the unicorn - the magic doesn't allow it. But when Aiden looks Crumb in the eye and BELIEVES, fully and without hedging, that there is a unicorn standing on the dock, his belief is strong enough to make her real for Crumb. Just for Crumb. Just for that moment.

That's a six-year-old's super power. He believes harder than any adult ever could.

After all seven other kids have done their parts, Mr. Crumb is shouting that everyone is making it up. He yells, "PROVE TO ME this is REAL MAGIC."

Aiden walks forward. He's the smallest one there.

He whistles.

Pegasus walks up the dock. The other kids see her. The eight kids see her. The other adults in the crowd don't - they just see Crumb's face change.

Crumb's mouth opens. His clipboards drop. He sees a unicorn that nobody else can see. He thinks he's losing his mind. He can't even SAY there's a unicorn because the other adults will think HE'S crazy.

Aiden says, "Mr. Crumb, this is Pegasus. She's a unicorn."

Pegasus says, "HI!"

Crumb whispers, "I... can't... I can't put a unicorn on a permit."

Aiden says, "We know."

The crowd can't see Pegasus, but they can see CRUMB - white-faced, sweating, broken. The deal is dead. Driedmore is going to need a different foreman.

If the reader picked Decision 4A, **the Hush is also at the showdown** - a tiny weird creature visible only to the kids, riding on Aiden's shoe. The kids share looks of "is this real?" all afternoon.

## Notes
- Aiden's branch is the most fairy-tale, the most read-aloud-friendly, and the most magical because he is the youngest and the most pure-hearted.
- Pegasus's "LOOK AT THIS!" is the running gag of the chapter.
- The Hush is a side character who could come back in a sequel - the lonely little creature who Aiden hugged.
- The unicorn coming back with Aiden is THE moment Crumb cannot recover from. The whole book builds to it.
