# CYOA Master Flow

This is the structural document for the book. The 8 branch files in `branches/` are now CHARACTER BIBLES - reference material for each kid (their trait, magical creature, earned thing). The actual book structure is below: one CYOA narrative with focal scenes scattered across the path. Reader follows 3 of 8 kids deeply per playthrough. Other 5 kids' adventures happen offscreen and get brief recaps at convergence.

---

## Math at a glance

- **Total book content:** ~21,000 words
- **Single playthrough:** ~14,000 words
- **Replay value:** 3-4 playthroughs to see every kid's focal scene
- **Reading time, single playthrough (3rd grader at 130 wpm):** ~1 hour 45 minutes, spread across 4-5 bedtime reads
- **Read-aloud to Aiden:** ~1 hour 40 minutes plus decision pauses, spread across 5-6 bedtime reads

---

## Scene network

### SCENE 0: Framing (always read - ~2,000 words)
Content per `framing.md`. Hotel Sun Lakes, Crumb's data center sign, dock foundation eroding, sunroom acts weird, C-130 thunders past, eight kids portal into The Old Roar.

### SCENE 1: Arrival in The Old Roar (always read - ~1,000 words)
All 8 kids land in a meadow at the foot of Dry Falls, which is WET and roaring in this world. The greeter is proposed as a friendly creature - working name **Tomby the Tortoise**, an ancient kid-friendly figure who explains the stakes in plain language.

Tomby tells them:
- The Old Roar is the ancient version of their lake. The two worlds are connected. If Crumb drains the lake, The Old Roar dries up too.
- Each kid needs to earn ONE thing from a different region of The Old Roar.
- They have until the next C-130 thunder (which in The Old Roar happens in about an Old-Roar-day).
- Eight regions, eight kids. Each kid is suited to one region.

The 8 kids look at each other. They split up and head off in eight directions.

### DECISION 1: Who to follow first?

Reader picks 1 of 8 focal scenes:

- **2A. Madison** at the Quiet Pool (Lake Sisters)
- **2B. Chayten** with the Driftwood Players (otter theater)
- **2C. Carson** at the Coulee Games (Big Coulee)
- **2D. Reid** in Old Speak's Wood (tree-creature)
- **2E. Kellen** at Skip-Stone Lake (bird-creatures)
- **2F. Keira** at the Painted Cliff (Cliffy)
- **2G. Ella** at the Bright Water Court (Queen Marigold)
- **2H. Aiden** in the Unicorn Meadow (Pegasus)

Each focal scene is ~1,400 words single-path with 1-2 internal flavor choices (small variation, no real branching - that complexity stayed at the kid-pick level).

After any focal scene, reader hits DECISION 2.

### FOCAL SCENES 2A-2H (~1,400 words each, reader reads 1-3 per playthrough)

Source material in `branches/` files. Each focal scene compresses the existing branch outline into a tighter single-path adventure. The kid:

1. Arrives in their region.
2. Meets their magical creature.
3. Has 1-2 small choices (flavor only, both lead forward).
4. Earns their ONE thing.
5. Heads to the gathering point.

The earned thing comes back with them. Allies (Branch the beaver, Big Coulee, Old Speak, etc.) appear at the showdown if the reader picked the warmest path within the focal scene.

### DECISION 2: Follow another kid OR head to the gathering point?

After a focal scene, reader picks:
- **Follow another kid** → back to DECISION 1, the kid you just followed is now off the menu
- **Head to the gathering point** → SCENE 3

Recommended: follow 3 kids before heading.

### SCENE 3: Convergence at the Gathering Hill (always read - ~600 words core + ~200-word recap per unfollowed kid)
All 8 kids meet at a high hill overlooking The Old Roar. Falls roaring in the distance. Brisket smoke faintly drifting up from the world above.

Each followed kid (the reader already saw their adventure) just nods and shows their earned thing.

Each UNFOLLOWED kid gives a brief 200-word recap of their adventure: "Mine was wild, you should have seen it - I'll tell you the whole thing later." Each recap mentions:
- The magical creature
- One specific funny moment
- The earned thing (visible)
- One detail that hooks the reader to follow this kid on a replay

The 8 kids look at each other and start walking back toward the meadow where Tomby waits.

### SCENE 4: Return through the sunroom (always read - ~500 words)
All 8 kids portal back. The rafts come back to plastic. Each kid is holding their earned thing (or, in Aiden's case, has Pegasus walking behind him - invisible to adults from the moment they cross over).

Pegasus says, "Is THIS the lake-up-above?"

The kids walk down to the dock together.

### SCENE 5: Showdown at the dock (always read - ~3,500 words)
Content per `showdown.md`. Eight beats, Crumb's bank-offer pushback, C-130 climax. Each beat references the earned thing whether the reader followed that kid or not - the showdown shows ALL 8 contributions because all 8 actually did them.

The depth of each beat varies based on whether the reader followed that kid:
- Followed kid: full beat as written.
- Unfollowed kid: slightly trimmed beat (still happens, fewer words).

This way every reader sees every kid contribute, but the kids they followed get more detailed showdown moments.

### SCENE 6: Coda (always read - ~500 words)
Sparklers, snap-its, brisket. Reid plants the Driftwood staff. Dock heals overnight. The kids share a look. The grown-ups don't see Pegasus.

- Plus internal flavor choices within each focal scene
- A book that lives at the cabin for many summers

## CYOA tree shape

```
SCENE 0: Framing (always)
   ↓
SCENE 1: Arrival (always)
   ↓
DECISION 1: Pick a kid (1 of 8)
   ↓
FOCAL SCENE (1 of 8 read)
   ↓
DECISION 2: Another kid or gathering hill?
   ↓                       ↓
DECISION 1 again       SCENE 3: Convergence (always)
(removed: kid you followed)         ↓
                               SCENE 4: Return (always)
                                       ↓
                               SCENE 5: Showdown (always)
                                       ↓
                               SCENE 6: Coda (always)
```

Recommended path: follow 3 kids before heading to gathering. Reader is welcome to follow more (longer playthrough, slightly less replay value) or fewer (shorter playthrough, more recap content at gathering).

---

## What changes from the previous outline

- The 8 branch files are no longer "chapters" - they're character bibles. The actual book has fewer, tighter scenes.
- Each kid's adventure compresses from ~2,500 words (previous chapter outline) to ~1,400 words (focal scene). The compression cuts the 5-decision-points-with-3-options-each structure down to 1-2 flavor choices per focal scene. The strongest material survives.
- Allies still show up at the showdown if the reader picked warm paths within the focal scene.
- The framing chapter is unchanged. The showdown is mostly unchanged (small trims for unfollowed-kid beats).
- Total book length drops from ~30,000 (with full hybrid branching) to ~21,000. Tighter book. More CYOA-like.
