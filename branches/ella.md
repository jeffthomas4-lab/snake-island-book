# Chapter 8 - Ella's Branch (8)

~2,000 words / 15-min read for a 3rd grader.

Lead trait: princess energy AND fierce protector of family. Both shine here. Regal courage.

## What she earns
**The Crown.** A small golden crown that gives her royal authority. At the showdown, she steps in front of Crumb and tells him NO in a voice that he cannot ignore. She also brings back the QUEEN'S PROMISE: that the Bright Water is now allies with Hotel Sun Lakes forever.

## The task (her prize quest)
Help a stressed-out kid-queen hold court for one afternoon and discover that she herself is also a queen.

## Intro (~300 words)

Ella's raft slides up to a glade with little white stones laid in a circle. Her raft has become a small boat with golden trim and a velvet seat. She steps onto the moss.

A FROG in a tiny crown bows to her.

"Princess," it says.

Ella says, "Princess?"

The frog says, "Your boat has GOLDEN TRIM. Only royalty have golden trim. We've been EXPECTING you."

Ella looks at her boat. It really does have golden trim.

She follows the frog into the glade. There are more frogs. There's a tiny throne in the middle, made of mossy stone. On the throne sits a girl Ella's age, wearing a crown that's slipping over one eye.

The girl is gripping the arms of the throne. Her knuckles are white.

The frog announces, "Princess Ella, daughter of the Lake-Up-Above, attending Her Majesty Queen Wren of the Bright Water."

(Yes, Wren. Like Madison's youngest Lake Sister. Same name, different girl - implies a connection between courts in The Old Roar. Possible callback for sequel.)

Wait, let me adjust the name to avoid confusion.

Queen MARIGOLD. Yes. Queen Marigold of the Bright Water. Better.

Marigold looks up. She is VERY stressed.

"Hi," Marigold whispers. "I've been queen for one week and I'm pretty sure I'm BAD at it. Please help."

Ella sits next to her on the throne. There's room for two.

Marigold cries, just a little.

The frogs erupt into argument. They are arguing about whether the new princess (Ella) should sit on the throne. Each frog has a different opinion. Each frog is LOUD.

Marigold whispers to Ella, "They argue about EVERYTHING."

## Decision 1: How to handle the arguing frogs

- **A. Tell them to be quiet.** (Direct. They're surprised. They go quiet.)
- **B. Make them argue with her instead, one at a time.** (Strategic - she controls the conversation.)
- **C. Ignore them and talk to Marigold.** (Best for the friendship. The frogs eventually quiet because no one's listening.)

## Decision 2: The first court case (Ella's fierce protector moment)

A fox-creature strides in. He's claiming part of the Bright Water as his territory. He has a SCROLL. He's very confident.

But behind him, hiding under a fern, is a tiny water-creature - the egg-mother from later. She's terrified. The fox isn't claiming abstract territory. He's claiming HER nest. He wants to take her eggs and her home.

Marigold doesn't see her at first. Ella does.

Ella STANDS UP from the throne.

She walks down the steps. She steps between the fox and the fern. She is eight years old and three feet tall. The fox is twice her size.

She says, "You will NOT take her home."

The fox blinks.

- **A. Ella reads the scroll out loud and shows it's a forgery.** (Smart. The fox is exposed in front of the whole court.)
- **B. Ella stares the fox down without saying another word.** (Princess fury. The fox actually steps back.)
- **C. Ella tells the fox he can have a different territory she's making up on the spot.** (Mercy with steel. The fox accepts because he's been beaten and he knows it.)

The fox's claim falls apart. He bows (still has manners) and leaves. Marigold is shaking. Ella turns and goes back to the throne like nothing happened. The frogs are SILENT. The whole court has just learned that this princess is not to be tested.

If the reader picked path B or C, **the egg-mother and her court SHOW UP at the showdown** as a chorus that condemns Crumb when he tries to bully Ella later.

## Decision 3: A real problem

A small water creature comes in. She's lost her egg. She's panicked. The court has to decide whether to send searchers, organize a hunt, or comfort the mother first.

- **A. Send searchers immediately.** (Practical.)
- **B. Comfort the mother.** (Princess Ella's instinct. She holds the mother's hand.)
- **C. Both - she organizes searchers AND stays with the mother.** (Strongest. Marigold watches and learns.)

The egg is found (by Branch the beaver, callback to Madison's branch - he's a recurring helper).

## Decision 4: Marigold has a meltdown

After three more cases, Marigold can't take it. She runs out of the throne room. Ella follows.

Marigold is sitting on a rock crying. She says she doesn't WANT to be queen anymore.

- **A. Tell her she doesn't have to be.** (She sniffles. Decides to try a little longer.)
- **B. Tell her she's already a great queen.** (Marigold blushes. She believes Ella.)
- **C. Tell her they can be queens TOGETHER for the rest of the day.** (Marigold lights up. Ella has invented co-ruling. **Marigold sends a frog herald to the showdown** who delivers a formal Bright Water alliance proclamation that announces Hotel Sun Lakes as protected territory.)

## Decision 5: Sundown - Marigold makes a gift

At sundown, Marigold takes off her crown. She has another one in her pocket. (She always carries two. She inherited them from her grandmother.)

She holds out the second crown to Ella.

- **A. Take the crown.** (Now Ella is officially a queen of the Bright Water.)
- **B. Refuse - tell Marigold it should stay in her family.** (Marigold puts it on Ella's head anyway.)
- **C. Take the crown and give Marigold something in return - the small mirror Ella has in her pocket.** (Now they're BOTH queens AND best friends.)

Each path makes Ella a queen of the Bright Water. The strongest path also gives Marigold the mirror, which becomes a sequel hook.

## Crescendo close (~300 words)

Marigold makes a speech to the court. The frogs are quiet for once.

She announces that Princess Ella is now a Queen of the Bright Water. The frogs cheer (in their froggy way). The court erupts in joy.

Marigold pulls Ella aside.

"Listen," she says. "Being a queen isn't about HAVING POWER. It's about USING POWER WISELY. You used power wisely all afternoon. You stood up for the egg-mother. You saw through the fox. You comforted me. THAT is what makes a queen."

She places the crown on Ella's head. It fits.

Ella says, "I'm not going to wear this in real life, am I?"

Marigold laughs. "It will be invisible to everyone except you. And me. And anyone who deserves to see royalty."

She gives Ella a kiss on each cheek (the formal way queens greet each other).

"If anyone EVER threatens your home," Marigold says, "you tell them you're a Queen of the Bright Water. And if they don't listen, you call my name. The Bright Water is your ally now. Forever."

Ella climbs back onto her boat. The golden trim glints in the sunset light.

Marigold waves from the moss circle. The frogs are arguing again behind her.

Ella laughs. She paddles away.

## How this feeds the showdown
Ella's COURAGE is what stops Crumb when he tries to bully her aside. The crown is symbolic. What actually moves him is an eight-year-old refusing to flinch.

He says, "Step aside, little girl."

Ella draws herself up to her full eight-year-old height. The crown becomes visible to him for one second. He sees it. He doesn't know what he's seeing.

She says, "I am Queen Ella of the Bright Water. And the answer is NO."

It's not the crown. It's her face. He knows that face from earlier today when he was trying to put up his sign. He recognized it then as a kid he could ignore. He recognizes it now as something else.

His hat falls off. The crowd applauds. He cannot recover from a small girl having said no.

If Decision 2B or 2C, **the egg-mother and her court appear** as a chorus condemning Crumb's plan.

If Decision 4C, **Marigold's frog herald arrives** with a formal alliance proclamation - the Bright Water has officially declared Hotel Sun Lakes protected territory.

## Notes
- Ella's princess energy and her protectiveness shine simultaneously. The branch celebrates both as one trait, not two competing things.
- Marigold and the Bright Water court are sequel-ready.
- Branch the beaver returns from Madison's branch as the egg-finder. Recurring helper.
- The crown being invisible-except-to-the-worthy is the magic.
