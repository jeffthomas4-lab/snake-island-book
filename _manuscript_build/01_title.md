# How to Read This Book

This is a *choose-your-own-adventure* book.

Start at **Passage 1** and read straight through until the Harbor Keeper sends the eight cousins off to their regions.

Then pick **one cousin** to follow first. Turn to the passage number listed for that cousin. Read their chapter. Inside the chapter there will be one bold choice with three options. Look for the **passage number** in the option you pick and turn to it.

When a cousin's chapter ends, the book will tell you what passages to turn to next. You can read the cousins in any order. When you have read as many as you want, turn to **Passage 400** to bring everyone home.

The **Showdown** is one long scene with eight beats, one per cousin. Each beat has two versions. Read the version that matches whether you turned to that cousin's chapter or not.

The book ends with one of two endings. You'll know which one when you get there.
