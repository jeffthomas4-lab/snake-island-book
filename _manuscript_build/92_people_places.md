## Friends You Meet Along the Way

**The Harbor Keeper.** Greets every cousin at the start of their region and waits for them when they come back.

**The Old Roar.** The lake itself. Some of the cousins are sure she remembers everything that has ever happened on her shore.

**Big Coulee.** The wooly mammoth Carson races, and the mother of Tuft and Tumble.

**Pip-Pip.** Big Coulee's small rabbit assistant. Always has a clipboard.

**Queen Marigold.** The frog who crowns Ella.

**Old Speak.** The grove that pays attention.

**Pegasus.** Visits the sunroom on full moons. Carries Aiden where he needs to go.

**The Three Sisters.** The dancers of the Quiet Pool, who have been waiting a long time for a real one.


## The Two Endings

**Crown.** Most of the cousins went deep, and Pegasus came back. The lake names the eight of you.

**Silver.** Some went deep, some took the quicker path. The lake remembers anyway.
